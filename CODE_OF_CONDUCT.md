# Code of conduct

This is a small project about card games and computer vision. These are the rules for
taking part in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What we ask

- **Be kind.** Write to people the way you would speak to them across a table.
- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Say what is wrong with the code, the label or the
  claim. Do not say what is wrong with whoever made it.
- **Assume a newcomer.** Many people here are not engineers. A question that seems basic to
  you is welcome.
- **Be honest about what you know.** Say whether you measured something or are guessing. Give
  the command and the output.
- **Take "no" well.** One person maintains this. A closed issue or a declined pull request is
  a decision about scope or time, not about you.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or sexual language or imagery.
- Attacks on anyone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information. That includes pointing others at a streamer, a
  player or a shop in order to bother them. The label manifests carry video ids so that
  frames can be found; they are not a list of people to contact.
- Bringing arguments about other projects or other people into this one.
- Posting card art, video frames or other people's footage here.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close or lock a thread, or block an account.
For a small first lapse you get a note asking you to stop. For anything serious, or
repeated, there is no warning.

## Reporting

Write to <riftsight.support@gmail.com>. Reports are read by the maintainer and are
kept private.

This project has one maintainer, so there is nobody else here to take a report about the
maintainer. For that, use the reporting tools of the site that hosts the repository.

## Scope

The rules apply in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere
someone speaks for the project.
