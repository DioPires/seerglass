# Names and logos

The Apache-2.0 license covers the code and this project's own work in the weights, and our
own work in the bank and identity groups, and the catalog's selection and arrangement. It does
not cover names or logos. Section 6 of the license says so.

## RiftSight

The name RiftSight and the RiftSight logo are not licensed by this repository.

- A fork, or a product built on this, must use its own name and its own logo.
- You may say, truthfully, that your work is "based on RiftSight's open vision model".
- Do not use the name or the logo in a way that suggests your work is RiftSight, or that
  RiftSight stands behind it.

The Python package is called `cardvision` so that a fork can rename the project without
renaming its imports. You may keep that package name.

## Riftbound

Riftbound, the card names and the card art belong to Riot Games. This repository holds card
names and card ids in `games/riftbound/catalog.json` so that the tools can report what they
see. It holds no card art.

This is a fan project. It is not endorsed by Riot Games.

## Other games

If you adapt the tools to another game, that game's name, card names and card art belong to
its publisher. Check what the publisher allows before you publish a catalog, a bank or
weights for it.
